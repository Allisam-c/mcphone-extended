# MCphone v1.12.1.1
<!-- date: 2026-11-25 -->

## 简体中文

### 修复

- 修复 Minecraft 26.3 副手手机 HUD 在 110% 等缩放比例下，右上角电池与信号图标被裁掉或消失的问题；裁剪区域现在与手机缩放保持一致。
- 同一裁剪修复也用于页面内容与导航图标，保留电池原有的靠右位置和自定义缩放。

## English

### Fixed

- Fixed the battery and signal icons being clipped or disappearing in the offhand phone HUD on Minecraft 26.3 at scales such as 110%. Clipping now follows the phone transform exactly once.
- Applied the same clipping correction to page content and navigation icons while preserving the battery's right alignment and custom HUD scaling.
